# CyberShield-EDU: 10-Pillar Strategic Roadmap 🛡️

This document outlines the strategic evolution of the CyberShield-EDU core platform, focusing on technical depth, expert forensics, and psychological education.

---

## 🏗️ Phase 1: Interactive Intelligence
### 1. 🧠 The "Security Tutor" (LLM Explainer)
- **Goal**: Move from binary "Scam/Safe" labels to educational dialogues.
- **Action**: Integrate an LLM to take raw forensic markers (GeoIP, Entropy, Pattern Matches) and explain the *psychological* tactics behind the scam.
- **Value**: Students don't just see a "Red Box"—they learn how the trap was set.
- **Status**: ⏸️ **INACTIVE / PAUSED**

### 2. 🧩 Advanced Forensic Pattern Engine
- **Goal**: Hardened heuristic detection (COMPLETED).
- **Action**: Real-time TLD analysis, full-path keyword scanning, and GeoIP enrichment.
- **Status**: ✅ **ACTIVE**

---

## 🤝 Phase 2: Trust & Institutional Safety
### 3. 🛠️ The "Safe Provider" Whitelist
- **Goal**: Institutional verification of internship/job sources.
- **Action**: Partner with university Career Centers to pre-verify legitimate domains.
- **Value**: Verified providers get a "Shield of Trust" badge, instantly separating them from the noise.

---

## 🕵️ Phase 5: Advanced Media Forensics (Pillar 5)
### 5. 🖼️ Generative AI & Deep-Fake Forensics
- **Goal**: Countering the next generation of social engineering.
- **Action**: EXIF metadata auditing, physical texture (Laplacian) analysis, and AI signature detection.
- **Status**: ✅ **ACTIVE**

## 🎓 Phase 6: Cyber-Academy Gamification (Pillar 6)
### 6. 🎮 Gamification & XP Persistence
- **Goal**: Turning education into a persistent, rewarding journey.
- **Action**: RPG-like progression (Cyber Scout to Grand Protector), badge milestones, and full database persistence.
- **Status**: ✅ **ACTIVE**

---

## ⚖️ Phase 7: Legal & System Correlation
### 7. ⚖️ Legal "Evidence Package" Generator
- **Goal**: Helping students take official action.
- **Action**: A "Generate Evidence Report" button that builds a signed PDF with all forensic data.

### 8. 🔌 Advanced PDF-URL Recursive Scanning
- **Goal**: Extracting threats buried inside document layers.
- **Action**: Automatically extract links found *inside* uploaded PDFs and run them through the URL scanner.

### 9. 🕵️ Multi-Vector Correlation (The Campaign Engine)
- **Goal**: Connecting the dots between different scans.
- **Action**: Logic that identifies when a URL, a PDF, and a text message are part of the same "Campaign."

---

## 📊 Phase 10: Institutional Governance
### 10. 📊 Deep Analytics Dashboard (Admin 2.0)
- **Goal**: Strategic insights for university IT.
- **Action**: Advanced data visualization for "Targeted Departments" and "Most Common Scam TACTICS."
